🦦 Wotto

Wotto is a little otter who reminds you to drink water. On your Mac he lives in the menu bar and hangs out on your desktop; on your iPhone he floats on your screen, your widgets, and the Dynamic Island. One purchase covers both, and they stay in sync through your own iCloud — log a glass on either, both know.

How to use Wotto

  • Click the 🦦 in the menu bar to log a glass and see your day.
  • Right-click it for quick actions (log, undo, hide Wotto, quit).
  • Poke the otter on your desktop. He likes it. Probably.
  • Set your wake hours, daily goal, and reminder pace in the panel.
  • Need discipline? Turn on "i need a push" — reminders fill the whole screen with rising water until you log a glass. Esc always works.
  • On iPhone: say "I drank water in Wotto" to Siri, glance at the Dynamic Island when a glass is due, or log straight from a widget.
