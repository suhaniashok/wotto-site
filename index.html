🦦 Wotto

Wotto is a desktop otter for your Mac who reminds you to drink water. He lives in your menu bar, hangs out on your desktop, gets a little sad when you forget to hydrate, and gives you a flower when you don't.

How to use Wotto

  • Click the 🦦 in the menu bar to log a glass and see your day.
  • Right-click it for quick actions (log, undo, hide Wotto, quit).
  • Poke the otter on your desktop. He likes it. Probably.
  • Set your wake hours, daily goal, and reminder pace in the panel.
  • Need discipline? Turn on "i need a push" — reminders fill the whole screen with rising water until you log a glass. Esc always works.
