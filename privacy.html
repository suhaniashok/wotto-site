🦦 Wotto — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

Wotto is a little desktop otter who reminds you to drink water. He is very good at that, and very bad at collecting data — because he doesn't collect any.

What Wotto collects

Nothing. Wotto has no accounts, no analytics, no trackers, and no ads. The app never connects to the internet.

Where your data lives

Your daily glass count, streak, and settings are stored in a single small file on your Mac, inside the app's own sandboxed container. That data never leaves your computer. Deleting the app deletes it.

Third parties

There are none. No data is shared with anyone, because no data exists to share.

Changes to this policy

If Wotto ever changes how he handles data, this page will be updated before that version ships.
